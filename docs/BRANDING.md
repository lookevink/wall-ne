# WALL-NE branding

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the user's robot-eye and squared wordmark references as design direction. PNG assets retain transparency. Native `sips` resizing produces web and touch-icon sizes; the ICO contains 16, 32 and 48 pixel PNG entries.

- `web/assets/brand/wall-ne-logo.png`: horizontal header logo, 960 × 320.
- `web/assets/brand/wall-ne-eyes.png`: eyes-only icon, 512 × 512.
- `web/favicon.ico`, `web/favicon-32.png`, `web/apple-touch-icon.png`: browser and home-screen icons.

## Final logo prompt

Create an original logo for a small agricultural robotics project named WALL-NE. Transparent background. Wide horizontal layout: two friendly mechanical binocular camera eyes at left, the exact word WALL-NE at right. Eyes look straight forward: perfect left-right mirror symmetry, identical warm gray rounded angular metal housings, identical dark concentric circular lenses, centers on same horizontal line, no neck or body. Clean simplified vector-like rendering. Text in bold wide squared geometric ivory capitals with a thin charcoal outline and subtle bevel, with NE contained in a red circular badge. Short hyphen between WALL and NE. Crisp and readable at small header size. Eye pair approximately as tall as lettering and badge. Small transparent margin. No additional text, scenery, or background.

## Final icon prompt

From this logo create ONLY its binocular camera eye pair as a square transparent app icon/favicon. Remove all the wordmark letters, hyphen, and red badge. Preserve the exact warm ivory-gray metal housings and dark lenses design. Eyes face straight forward, identical sizes with centers on same horizontal line, make the right eye an exact horizontal mirror of the left including reflections so the entire eye pair has perfect bilateral mirror symmetry. Scale the pair to occupy 94 percent of the square canvas width and center vertically, no body, no neck, no text, no border or background. Simplify tiny details slightly to be clear at 32px. Real alpha transparency.
